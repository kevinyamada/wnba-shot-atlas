How to read the comparison

The original study counted 38,535 attempts and 38,533 field-goal points in 286 regular-season games. Excluding 94 beyond-half-court attempts worth zero points leaves 38,441 shots. Free throws are excluded.

For each region, the mix contribution is the team’s difference from league shot share multiplied by the difference between the regional and overall league scoring rates. The within-region contribution is the team’s share times its scoring-rate difference from the league. Add both columns to the league baseline to recover observed scoring. Rounding can change a total by 0.01.

The ranges resample 286 entire games 2,000 times, keeping opponents together and recalculating league rates. Within-region scoring combines shooting ability, difficulty, defender pressure and exact location. This comparison describes the season and cannot establish the payoff from changing shot selection.

What if only the other 12 teams set the rates?

Each shot gets the average field-goal points scored by the other teams in the same four-foot square and region. When those teams took no shots there, we use their rate in the surrounding region. Across the 13 teams, 18 of 38,441 attempts needed that fallback. The baseline also excludes the selected team.
