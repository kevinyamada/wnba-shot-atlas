Where the numbers come from

What the source covers

See the audit by season

WNBA Stats provides the shots from 1997 to 2001. ESPN provides the later shots and schedules. From 2003, the audit counts games whose shots agree with both teams’ box scores. The explorer includes disagreements as recorded.

SeasonGames with shotsGames matching both boxesAttemptsUnknown location

Free throws, exhibition games, and the Commissioner’s Cup final are separate. Cup qualifying games count in the regular season. Shots from the other half stay in the totals and can be selected as an area. The map uses the three point line from each season. The early arc is shown in front of the rim, where its distance is documented. Attempts recorded exactly at the basket through 2010 stay in the totals with an unknown location. A scored row without a shot label counts as a made basket only where the team box confirms it.
