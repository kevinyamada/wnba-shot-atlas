    Shot locations

    Just inside the three point line

    There are far fewer attempts just inside the three point line than just outside it. The count difference is consistent with the extra reward beyond the line; the archive cannot establish why a player chose a location.

    8×higher shot density in the two foot band outside the line

    508 attempts inside versus 4,380 outside, adjusted for area. Game bootstrap interval: 7.25 to 8.82× (2,000 resamples). This describes shot selection; it does not prove that players stepped backwards.

    Model comparisons

    How well do the models predict makes?

    A Bayesian model shares information between nearby locations. Player effects let shooters differ, while keeping estimates for players with few shots closer to the league distribution.

    The selected four chain GP passes the sampling thresholds. Those checks assess the computation; game benchmarks assess predictions. All methods use the same eligible shots after excluding eight games with conflicting shooter attempt totals.

    Observed scoring outcomes

    What about expected field goal points?

    The archive gives scored points but lacks authoritative two or three point attempt values for every miss. We model three observed outcomes: 0, 2, and 3 points. This keeps make probability equal to P(2) + P(3), and expected points equal to 2P(2) + 3P(3).

    ModelPoints RMSEPoints MAE

    Uses the evaluation period selected above. Lower is better. These estimates describe the observed outcome mix conditional on taking a shot; they do not estimate the value of choosing a different shot or a future possession.

    Spatial validation

    Testing on a different part of the court

    Predicting a new game and predicting in an unseen area are different tasks. For the spatial check, we hold out one of four court regions, then remove training shots close to the held out region. Increasing this buffer makes the test stricter.

    Notes on the methods

    Bayesian models. A prior says which surfaces are plausible before observing shots. The binomial likelihood describes makes out of attempts. Their combination gives a posterior distribution.

    IDW and kriging. IDW averages nearby estimated rates. Kriging uses spatial covariance; universal kriging also includes a distance trend. These are comparison methods here, not full Bayesian shot models.

    SPDE and the mesh. A smooth random field is represented by values on a triangular mesh. Our binomial SPDE fit uses a fast conditional Laplace approximation. Its uncertainty is narrower in scope than the GP's full posterior.

    Ensemble. Average the distance baseline, SPDE, Bayesian GP, CatBoost context, and XGBoost context predictions with equal weights. A separate calibrated version fits an intercept and slope on validation only.

    Coverage, calibration and interpretation

    The score and control checks exclude special free throw windows, overturned reviews, inconsistent clocks or scores, and uncertain ownership. Quality is determined retrospectively. The remaining sample covers 52.7% of periods and can differ from the excluded games. Every retained score margin agrees with the source snapshot. Sixteen intervals end at a jump ball change without an intermediate recorded event; their reward is zero and a separate official possession is not established. Post event states use only information recorded up to that event.

    What remains beyond these data

    The event based possession value prototype is complete. Full spatial expected threat needs nonshot action locations and outcomes that this archive does not record. A play by play feed does not capture every pass and movement, so ten rows are not necessarily ten basketball actions. A fresh season evaluation also requires new supplied WNBA data.

    This is an exploratory WNBA study, not an official league product or a definitive player ranking. Every basketball input comes from the supplied Data (1).zip. All 45 files were inventoried. All 624 game/team shot totals and final scores agree with its boxes. The eight player attribution conflicts remain documented and excluded from the player context shot models; the team level possession prototype applies its own strict score and control checks. Raw tracking and defensive pressure are not available.
