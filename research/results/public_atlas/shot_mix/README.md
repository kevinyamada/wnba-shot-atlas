# Shot mix and conversion

Field goal points per shot rose from 0.911 in 1997 to 2001 to 1.003 in 2022 to 2026, a change of +0.092. Holding conversion at the average of the two periods, the change in where shots came from accounts for +0.015 (95% game resampling interval +0.013 to +0.017). Holding the shot mix at the average of the two periods, the change in points per shot within areas accounts for +0.077 (+0.069 to +0.086). Shot mix is 16% of the change (14% to 19%).

A change in conversion alone cannot establish why players scored more from an area. Defense, rules, shot selection within an area and how scorers record locations can all contribute.

## Areas

Near the basket covers mapped shots within 10 ft of the rim and shots recorded exactly at the basket through 2010. Midrange is inside the three point line and 10 ft or more from the rim. The three point area is outside the line used in that season. Other or unknown covers the other half and the remaining shots without a usable location.

## Method

For each area, shot share is its attempts divided by all attempts in the period, and points per shot is its field goal points divided by its attempts. Counts are pooled before any rate is computed. The mix part is the sum over areas of the change in share times the average points per shot of the two periods. Area rows show that product relative to the overall average, so an area adds to the mix part when it gains share and scores above average or loses share and scores below it. The conversion part is the sum of the average share times the change in points per shot. The two parts add up to the change exactly. Intervals resample whole games within each season 2,000 times with seed 20261123. They describe game to game variation and leave out uncertainty from how locations were recorded. The season file also gives points per shot with the mix held at the 30 season average and with conversion held at the 30 season average. A version based on the recorded attempt value instead of location is not possible, because ESPN misses in recent seasons do not say whether the attempt was a two or a three.
